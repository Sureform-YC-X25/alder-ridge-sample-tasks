# Data provenance and rights statement

The tasks and source files in this package were authored by domain experts and
informed by Sureform's partner engagements with a specialty mechanical
contractor. AI-assisted tools were used to refine expert-authored tasks and to
create privacy-preserving proxies of source data used to seed the simulated
company environment.

Raw base data supplied through partner work was handled under the applicable
commercial and confidentiality arrangements. Private and identifying content
was removed, replaced, or consistently pseudonymized before inclusion. Alder
Ridge Mechanical and its personnel, counterparties, communications,
transactions, account identifiers, signatures, and records are simulated.

The package does not contain identifiable client, partner, employee,
insurance, banking, or customer data. Rights to evaluate or use this package
are governed by the repository license and any separate written agreement with
Sureform. Broader training or production rights are not implied by repository
access.
